Δ_A is the inner reading's clean-target loss minus head A's; a its agreement with head A; G = Δ_A(rw-b1) − Δ_A(sub20) the shortfall; F = (Δ_A(rw-b1) − Δ_A(rw-oracle)) / G the recovered fraction; s the random targets' share of the summed weight at the end of training; P = 1 − s(rw-oracle) / s(rw-b1) the share removed; C = Δ_A(sub20-oracle) − Δ_A(sub20-rw) the clean-target cost of head A's judgement against head B's own; â the clean-target ladder's agreement at the model's Δ_A; b = â − a. Intervals are Bonferroni-corrected t-intervals at 98.75%.

This is the calibration run of the preregistration's calibration step: one seed, on windows the real run does not use. It decides nothing but whether each exploratory arm runs, and no verdict is computed. `rw-oracle` is N15's calibration model of the same seed, re-measured.
