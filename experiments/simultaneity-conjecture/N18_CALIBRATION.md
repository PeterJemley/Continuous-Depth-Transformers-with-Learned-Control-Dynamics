Generated by `n18_analyze.py` from the records in `n18_calib/`, with N12's `n80` records in `n12_calib/`, N13's `n80-rw` records in `n13_calib/`, N14's training records in `n14_calib/` and the saved `rw-oracle`'s training records in `n15_calib/`. Preregistration: `N18_PREREGISTRATION.md`.
Measured on the held-out tail's windows [513, 1024], N12's. Δ_A is head B's clean-target loss minus head A's. R = (Δ_A(rw-cloop) − Δ_A(rw-ctwin)) / (Δ_A(rw-peer) − Δ_A(rw-j20)), the loop's cost to head B on clean targets as a share of the peer's gap to rw-j20; W = (Δ_A(rw-ctwin) − Δ_A(rw-j20)) / the same gap, the weighting's share without the loop. G = Δ_A(rw-b1) − Δ_A(sub20); F = (Δ_A(rw-b1) − Δ_A(arm)) / G; x a judge's plain cross-entropy on the clean targets minus head A's; x_m its mixture-form loss minus head A's copy's. The claim's interval is a two-sided 95% t-interval; every other interval is a 95% t-interval and decides nothing.

This is the calibration run of the preregistration's calibration step: one seed, on windows the real run does not use. It may change nothing in the design, and no verdict is computed. `rw-oracle` is N15's calibration model of the same seed, re-measured; the loop-free line (S8) runs through N17's five calibration rungs.
