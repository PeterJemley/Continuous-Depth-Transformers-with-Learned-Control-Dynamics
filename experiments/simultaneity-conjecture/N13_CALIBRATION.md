Generated by `n13_analyze.py` from the records in `n13_calib/`, compared with N12's in `n12_calib/`. Preregistration: `N13_PREREGISTRATION.md`.
Measured on the held-out tail's windows [513, 1024], N12's. Δ_A is head B's inner softmax's clean-target loss minus head A's, within a model; S is N12's share of the gap closed, against the same seed's n80 yardstick.

This is the calibration run of the preregistration's calibration step: one seed, on windows the real run does not use. It decides nothing, and no verdict is computed. Precondition 3, the replicate floor, is the check it exists for.
